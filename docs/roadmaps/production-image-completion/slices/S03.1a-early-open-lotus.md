# S03.1a — Early-open lotus

**Objective:** Create one isolated early-open lotus cutout.

**Target basename:** `lotus-bloom-early-open`.

## Acceptance

- One complete flower, no stem or neighboring object unless composition requires a short natural base.
- Clearly more open than the existing bud and less open than the half-open state.
- Minimum useful master target: 1200 × 1200.
- Real alpha with transparent corners, clean pale-petal edges, and no color fringe.
- WebP at or below 250 KB where visual quality permits.
