# S03.2a — Stem and sepals

**Objective:** Provide a reusable botanical base for layered lotus states.

**Targets:** `lotus-stem`, `lotus-sepals`.

## Acceptance

- Stem and sepals are independently positionable or documented as one deliberately combined layer.
- Lighting and green tones match the approved lotus family.
- Alpha preserves fine edges without opaque halos.
- The evidence records intended transform origin and composition role.
