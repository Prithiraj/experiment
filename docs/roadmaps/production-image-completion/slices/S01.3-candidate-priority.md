# S01.3 — Candidate priority and extraction decision

**Objective:** Establish the one-at-a-time execution order and decide whether each candidate is retained, converted, extracted, generated, or deferred.

## Preflight

Inspect source boards at full resolution. Extraction is allowed only for a complete isolated subject with sufficient pixels, no grid/caption/neighbor contamination, and removable background without petal or stem loss.

## Acceptance

- Priority A completes the bloom chapter before atmosphere or editorial expansion.
- No panel is marked `extract` unless all contact-sheet gates pass.
- Generation candidates are sequenced individually.
- The review is recorded in `evidence/S01-ledger-review.md`.
