Generate or derive one asset at a time. Before the next asset begins, require a PNG master, matching optimized WebP, clean-alpha inspection when applicable, provenance, manifest-ready metadata, and an approved scene role.

Every required child is verified or explicitly deferred with a reason; no contact sheet is shipped.
