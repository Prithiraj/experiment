# S03 review — Individual lotus and water components

## Delivered

- Early-open lotus cutout plus optimized WebP.
- Half-open lotus cutout plus optimized WebP.
- Responsive reflection plate.
- Responsive screen-blend ripple overlay.
- Preserved generation sources for every new asset.

Every delivered file passed its dimensional, payload, and visual contract. Lotus alpha was checked on light and dark backgrounds; the four flower states form a clear progression. The water overlay was composited over the reflection plate to verify its blend contract.

## Deferred by design

Independent stem/sepals, inner structure, pollen, layered petals, extra leaf angles, and extra atmospheric overlays have no approved owning scene. Their stable candidate records remain available for later reopening.

**Decision:** Close `S03`; release `S04.1`.
